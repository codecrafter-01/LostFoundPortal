SLIDE 2 OF 6
Problem Statement & Proposed Solution
❌ Manual Challenges
  • Unorganized physical notice boards and WhatsApp chat groups.
  • Delayed communication leading to unrecovered items.
  • Lack of verification to confirm item ownership.
  • No automated status tracking for lost inquiries.
✅ Vignan Portal Solution
  • Single centralized web portal for all Vignan departments.
  • Real-time automated Smart Match Engine.
  • Instant Nodemailer email alerts on match detection.
  • Secure student profiles & JWT authenticated access.

Database Layer MongoDB Atlas Cloud, Mongoose 9 ODM
